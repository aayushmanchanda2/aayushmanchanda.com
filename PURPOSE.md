# Purpose

What this site is for, and the bar anything added to it has to clear.

## Mission

Publish what I actually ran, with the date it was true.

I use AI to build things for my companies and my clients. Most of what gets
written about these tools comes from people who read the launch post. I want one
place where the opinion came from someone who installed it, tried to ship real
work with it, and wrote down what happened.

Test it on my own work. Say what it did. Put the date on it.

## Vision

Worth one person's time.

As long as it helps one person, as long as it gives one person something they
can use, it is doing the job. Not an audience, not a funnel. Someone decides
faster because they read something honest here. That is a low bar and I intend
to keep it.

Don't wait for life to happen to you. You happen to life. This site is that
pointed at the work: the notes already exist on my machine, and public is just
where I keep them now.

## The bar

Anything added here has to pass all five.

1. **It earns its place.** Writing here is for the reader, not to show my work.
   A sentence whose job is to prove I thought about something goes.
2. **Tested, or it says it is not.** A saved link says saved. Only work I ran
   gets a verdict.
3. **Dated.** Every opinion carries the day it was last true. An old date is a
   warning, not a badge.
4. **Accurate without being defensive.** Say the true thing plainly, then stop.
   No disclaimers managing what you think of me.
5. **Warm, direct, forward-looking.** Confident, and never a sales voice.

The voice these are written in is `voice-guide-for-site.md`, in the research
notes for this repo. Read it before writing copy. `design.md` §6 is the rule set
it feeds.
